Vector Map Level 0 (VMAP0), edition 5
=====================================

Producer: National Imagery and Mapping Agency (NIMA), now the National
Geospatial-Intelligence Agency (NGA), United States.
Edition 5, 30 September 2000. Four libraries: NOAMER (North America),
EURNASIA (Europe and North Asia), SOAMAFR (South America, Africa and
Antarctica) and SASAUS (South Asia and Australia).

Terms, as the data state them:
- Releasability (database header table): UNRESTRICTED.
- Access constraints (FGDC metadata, FGDC_DAT.TXT): None.
- Use constraints (FGDC metadata): Cite National Imagery and Mapping
  Agency.

Terra Soar credits it as "Vector Map Level 0 (VMAP0), NGA".

Certain features of the Boundaries coverage and of the Reference library
are the intellectual property of Environmental Systems Research
Institute, Inc. (ESRI) and its licensors, under the ESRI Data License
Agreement in README1.TXT (internal or non-commercial use only): first
order administrative boundaries (bnd edge features with f_code FA000 and
USE 26), named administrative areas (bnd faces with f_code FA001 and a
name), and the Reference library's libref, placenam and polbnd
coverages. Terra Soar's VMAP0 packs leave all of them out, except the
separate "vmap0-admin" pack, which holds the first two under ESRI's
terms.

VMAP0 is a 1:1,000,000 product compiled mostly from the Operational
Navigation Charts of the 1970s to 1990s. Its boundaries, names and
features are of that time and of that accuracy (about 2 km); they are no
statement of any government's position on boundaries or names, and they
are not suitable for navigation.

The source files' own README1.TXT and FGDC_DAT.TXT are included next to
this notice, unchanged.

The source as downloaded: https://geo.lib.umn.edu/gisdata/vmap0/ (the
University of Minnesota's copy of the four CD-ROMs; NGA no longer
publishes VMAP0).
